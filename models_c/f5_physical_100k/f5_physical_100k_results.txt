Interpretation
--------------
- All 100,000 measured physical MCU inference calls matched the expected decisions.
- No parity failure was observed.
- Mean inference latency was approximately 1.835 ms at 84 MHz.
- Maximum observed inference latency was approximately 1.977 ms.
- The maximum value above is an observed maximum, not a formal WCET bound.
- The 100,000 runs are repeated executions over 8 fixed validation vectors,
  not 100,000 distinct CAN samples.
- The 892-byte value is static RAM measured by the benchmark plus observed
  stack high-water; it is not claimed as an absolute whole-system peak RAM bound.
