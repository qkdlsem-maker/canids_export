What this proves

Frozen V2 was evaluated on an external CAN dataset that was not used for model training or threshold selection.

Observed limitation

External zero-day generalization remains limited: false positives are low, but attack recall is modest. ROAD results are therefore presented as validation evidence, not as a Live Demo success metric.

Separation from Live Demo: ROAD 수치는 전체 frozen V2 외부평가 결과이며, 위 Live Demo replay의 프레임별 결과와는 별도의 실험입니다.
